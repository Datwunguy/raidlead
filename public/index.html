WoW Sync
▾
One-time setup: Download RaidLead and copy it into ...\_retail_\Interface\AddOns\.

Then download RaidLead Companion and install it — Windows might try to block it, but it's safe to run. Open it and click Log In; a page opens here asking you to approve it. Once approved, that's it — no folders to pick, nothing else to configure.
Night to night, once set up: RaidLead Companion stays running in the background (it starts with Windows automatically) and syncs everything on its own — your published roster goes to the game, and loot comes back here, with nothing to click on either side. Just /reload in-game after publishing a new roster to see it.
