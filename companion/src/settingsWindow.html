RaidLead Companion

Reads your addon's loot data and writes your published roster into WoW, syncing with your RaidLead account in the background — no bridge folder, no manual steps. Logging in opens your browser to approve this app once; after that it runs quietly on its own.

Browse to ...\_retail_\Interface\AddOns\ (the same folder you copied the RaidLead addon into) — this app works out your WoW install from it. Which account is yours is re-detected automatically every time, so there's nothing to pick.
There's no reliable way on Windows to launch an app specifically when a game starts without the same fragility this project already moved away from (a background Task Scheduler job) -- this is the closest, safe equivalent: it opens no window and does nothing at all until it detects WoW is actually running.
Loot exports automatically whenever the addon's SavedVariables file changes (i.e. after a /reload or logout). Roster imports automatically whenever the website queues a new one.
